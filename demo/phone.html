How is the score calculated?

The demo starts at 100 and subtracts penalties for estimated UV, an unlogged/due sunscreen reminder, heart rate, SpO₂ and sensor temperature. A further 12 points may be subtracted when certain UV and heart-rate or temperature rules occur together.

Breakdown--

Breakdown values are rounded individually for display.

80–100 Optimal · 55–79 Caution · 0–54 High Risk. If heart rate, SpO₂ or temperature is missing, the score is No Data. This is not a clinical score or a safe-exposure calculation.

Type multipliers for this unvalidated demo: I 1.25, II 1.20, III 1.15, IV 1.10, V 1.05, VI 1.00. Not sure defaults to 1.25. Sensitive mode multiplies UV by a further 1.35. All skin types need sun protection.

Watch time

When connected, the browser sends its local time to the ESP32. The watch keeps time while powered, without an external clock module. Re-sync after restart or power loss; it may drift.

Your wiring

UV GPIO 0 · TP4056 divider GPIO 1 · DS18B20 GPIO 3 · touch GPIO 4 · motor driver GPIO 5 · I²C SDA GPIO 8 / SCL GPIO 9 · buzzer GPIO 10.

GPIO 8 and 9 are boot-strapping pins. Check bus pull-ups, boot behaviour and your board's onboard LED. See the hardware guide before powering the circuit.
