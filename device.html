02 THE COMPOSITE SCORE

Different signals.
One overview.

An illustrative rule set starts at 100 and subtracts points when its example conditions are met.

UV, sunscreen timing, heart rate, SpO₂ and sensor temperature all play a part. Choose a scenario to see how the demo changes.

What goes into the score? +

UV, sunscreen timing and readings outside selected thresholds can add penalties. Skin type and sensitivity adjust an illustrative UV penalty. Scores 80–100 are labelled Optimal, 55–79 Caution, and below 55 High Risk by these rules. Missing valid heart-rate, SpO₂ or temperature values show No Data.

This is a demo rule set, not a diagnosis or medical advice. The current firmware contains the complete formula.

How do the skin and sensitivity settings work? +

The app supports a self-reported skin type and a separate Standard or Sensitive setting. These adjust illustrative UV rules; they are not validated personalised protection advice or safe-exposure limits.

When can I see an alert on the phone? +

The watch runs its experimental alert rules locally. Phone alerts depend on the app page being open and connected. Readings and prompts should not be relied on for health or safety decisions.

How should I charge the prototype? +

Remove the device, switch the battery OFF and unplug ESP32 USB before charging. Unplug the charger before switching the battery ON. Do not charge unattended; stop if the battery becomes hot, swollen or damaged.
